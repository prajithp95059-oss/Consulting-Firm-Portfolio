How an engagement runs

Four stages, from first call to measured outcome.

01

Diagnose

A focused two-to-three week diagnostic to find the real constraint — not the one that's easiest to talk about.

02

Design

We build the plan with your team in the room, so it's shaped by the constraints you actually operate under.

03

Implement

We stay through execution when you want us to — running workstreams alongside your team, not handing off a binder.

04

Measure

Every engagement ends with a defined set of metrics we track together, so "did it work" has a real answer.
