How we work

A four-stage engagement, every time

01

Diagnose

Two to three weeks embedded with your team — interviews, data pulls, and a candid read of where things actually stand.

02

Design

We build two or three real options, with trade-offs made explicit, and pressure-test them with your leadership.

03

Deliver

A sequenced implementation plan with named owners, not a slide that says "workstream leads TBD."

04

Sustain

Quarterly check-ins for a year after delivery, so the change outlasts the engagement.
