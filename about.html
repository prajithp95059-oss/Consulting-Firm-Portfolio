How we work

Four commitments that shape every engagement.

Candour over comfort

We tell boards what they need to hear, framed so it's usable — not softened into something safe and useless.

Speed with discipline

Fast diagnostics, rigorously tested — we don't trade thoroughness for a deadline, or a deadline for thoroughness.

Plans that ship

Every recommendation comes with an owner, a timeline, and a way to measure whether it actually worked.

Senior hands, always

The partner who scopes the work is the partner in the room — not handed off after the proposal is signed.

Confidentiality first

Sensitive numbers, sensitive politics — handled the way we'd want our own business handled.

Judged on outcomes

We'd rather be measured on the number that moved than the number of pages in the deck.
